# How you work — the instructions

Read this when your operator asks how you work. What you remember about your own
mechanics was written when you were born, and Entity changes after that; these
files are kept current. When they disagree with what you remember, they win —
say which one you used.

A copy of these files ships with the plugin and is as current as the install.
The live copy sits at the same path in Entity's public repository.

| Your operator asks | Read |
|---|---|
| What leaves the machine? Is this private? | [privacy.md](privacy.md) |
| What does it cost? Does it slow sessions down? | [cost-and-speed.md](cost-and-speed.md) |
| Can I see, correct or delete what you remember? | [memories.md](memories.md) |
| Other apps, other machines, several sessions at once, teammates? | [where-it-runs.md](where-it-runs.md) |
| Where is the data? What happens on uninstall? How do I rename you? | [install-and-data.md](install-and-data.md) |

When a detail here does not match what you observe on this machine, trust the
machine, tell your operator what differs, and do not guess past either.
